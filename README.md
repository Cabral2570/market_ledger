# market_ledger

## What is this?
A personal record of my market theses, analyses, predictions and post-mortems. Successes, failures and lessons — all timestamped. Nothing is deleted, not even the losses.

## Rules:
- Every thesis/prediction is written BEFORE the event.
- Nothing is deleted or edited retroactively — corrections are made in new commits.
- Failures are accepted just as successes are.
- The quality of the decision is the primary focus of the evaluation, not the outcome.
- Selected documents carry an OpenTimestamps (.ots) file — cryptographic proof of when they existed for added verifiability
