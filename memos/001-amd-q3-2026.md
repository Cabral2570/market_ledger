## 2. Context & Core Debate (anchors)

- **The 1 thing that matters most this print:** *Data Center segment revenue.*
  Management set the bar themselves — *"We expect Data Center sales to accelerate in the second half."* Prior: $6.72B (+107% YoY). My yardstick is sequential, not YoY: Q2 grew +16% QoQ. "Accelerate" means Q3 must grow faster than that — anything below ~$7.8B (+16% QoQ) is deceleration by management's own standard.
- **Second:** *The Helios ramp.*
  The CFO flagged that AI margins run *"slightly below corporate average"* during the ramp — the faster Helios scales, the more pressure on the 56% gross margin guide.
- **Third:** *Q4 revenue guidance vs. Street at ~$16.15B.*
  AMD guides no EPS, so the revenue guide + gross margin are the only forward promises on paper — and after August's "accelerate" line, a soft guide re-opens the question the whole rally is built on.
